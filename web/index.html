InvisibleWatermark

Hide a short text in the pixels of an image and read it back. Images are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Image

PNG, JPEG, BMP or WebP. You can also drop a file anywhere on this page.

Embed

The mark becomes faintly visible on flat areas such as a clear sky, and the text is limited to 32 bytes.
